At export, `Utils.addFormatedText` (`Resources/scripts/commands/file.js`) adds a `formatInfo` to each cell style:  - `text`: the value formatted by SpreadJS (`GC.Spread.Formatter.GeneralFormatter.format`);  - or `content`: the parts of the format, when it has a filling character (`*x`).  `svg_drawCellValue` then draws that text, aligned on the cell.- In a number format, `_x` means "an invisible gap the width of `x`", and `?` a gap the width of a digit. For example, currency `$#,##0.00_);($#,##0.00)` keeps a gap the width of `)` after positive numbers, so that they line up with negatives in parentheses. Accounting does the same with `_(` / `_)`.- The formatted string from SpreadJS has a single space for each `_x` / `?`, but the SpreadJS canvas leaves a gap the width of the character. In the SVG the space is trimmed, and even kept it would be narrower than `)`. So positive values were drawn too far right compared to negatives and to the area: the decimal points were not aligned.- **The July 2024 attempt:** it added a trailing space and made spaces significant (`white-space: pre`). It was reverted: still not aligned (QA, Windows with Calibri), because a space is not the width of `)`, and it changed spacing in the whole SVG.### FixThe idea: send the gap characters to 4D separately and measure their real width with the cell font.1. **JS, export:** after formatting, `_addPlaceholderInfo(result, formatInfo.content)` looks at the format parts:   - it collects the leading and trailing placeholders (`placeholder` = `_x` gives `x`, `numberPlaceholder` = `?` gives `0`);   - it removes the matching spaces from `formatInfo.text`;   - it sets `formatInfo.placeholderLeft` / `formatInfo.placeholderRight` (for example `")"`).   Example: `1234.5` with the currency format gives `{text: "$1,234.50", placeholderRight: ")"}`, and `-1234.5` gives `{text: "($1,234.50)"}`.2. **4D, `VP Convert to picture`:**   - computes `$paddingLeft` / `$paddingRight` with `svg_getTextWidth(<chars>; $parsedStyle.fontObj)`, only for non-wrapped, non-vertical, non-rotated text;   - adds them to the text width for the `####` overflow test (they are reset to 0 when `####` is drawn), and scales them like the font with shrink to fit;   - **filling-character formats (`content` path):** `?` and `_x` now count in the width that reduces the fill (before, they were ignored), and the leading gaps (before part1) and trailing gaps (after part2) are measured too;   - passes `paddingLeft` / `paddingRight` in `$valObj`.3. **4D, `svg_drawCellValue`:** shifts the text x position:   - left: `x+2+paddingLeft`;   - center: `+(paddingLeft-paddingRight)/2`;   - right: `x+width-2-paddingRight`;   - two-part filling formats: part1 starts after `paddingLeft` and part2 ends before `paddingRight`.Files: `Resources/scripts/commands/file.js`, `Project/Sources/Methods/VP Convert to picture.4dm`, `Project/Sources/Methods/svg_drawCellValue.4dm`.### PatchApplies alone on main, and also before or after the ACI0105646 patch (they touch
